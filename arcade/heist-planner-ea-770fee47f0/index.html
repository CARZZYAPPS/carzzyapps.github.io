How to play

Plan it. Run it. Fix it.

  • 1Plot routes. Tap a tile to send the selected crew member there, or drag from the glowing ring to draw a path tile by tile. Every step is one tick.
  • ⏱Wait adds a one-tick pause. Hold it to add several. Timing is everything.
  • ⇆Drag the timeline to preview the whole job at any tick: guards, camera sweeps and lasers move exactly as they will on the run.
  • ◣Red and orange tiles are being watched by guards and cameras. Dark hatched shadows hide you from cameras. Guards only spot you there if they're right on top of you. Lasers only bite while lit.
  • ✂Trim cuts the selected crew's plan at the playhead, so you can fix the part that went wrong and keep the rest.
  • ★Three stars: get out clean, take every bonus, and finish at or under par. Only improvements to your best score pay out Lazer Points.
Keyboard: Space play/stop · W wait · A action · Z/Y undo/redo · X trim · 1–4 crew · ←→ scrub
