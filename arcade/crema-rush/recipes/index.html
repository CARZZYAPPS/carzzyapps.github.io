No espresso machine? No problem.

For espresso: a moka pot or an AeroPress makes a strong, short coffee that works in every drink below. Use about 2 oz of it for each double shot.

For steamed milk: heat the milk until it steams, without boiling. Then shake it in a sealed jar (fill it halfway) for 30 seconds, or pump it in a French press until it doubles. Let it sit for a few seconds so the foam settles on top.

Espresso

Hot⏱ About 30 secondsMakes 1 shot (2 oz)
In Crema Rush · Level 1Espresso machine

You need

  • 18 g espresso beans, ground fine (like table salt)
  • Filtered water, heated to about 200°F (93°C)

Steps

  1. Grind the beans right before brewing and dose 18 g into the basket.
  2. Level the grounds and tamp firmly and evenly.
  3. Lock in the portafilter and start the shot right away.
  4. Stop at about 36 g (2 oz) of liquid, 25 to 30 seconds after you start.
  5. Drink it within a minute or two, before the crema fades.

Tip Too sour and fast? Grind finer. Bitter and slow? Grind coarser. Change one thing at a time.

Latte

Hot⏱ 5 minutesMakes 1 drink (10 to 12 oz)
In Crema Rush · Level 1Espresso machine + Milk steamer

You need

  • 1 double shot of espresso (2 oz)
  • 6 to 8 oz cold whole milk

Steps

  1. Pull a double shot straight into a warm 12 oz cup.
  2. Steam the milk: tip of the wand just under the surface for 3 to 5 seconds to add air, then sink it deeper to spin and heat.
  3. Stop at about 140 to 150°F (60 to 65°C), when the jug is hot but you can still hold it.
  4. Tap and swirl the jug until the milk looks glossy, like wet paint.
  5. Pour into the espresso from a little height, then lower the jug at the end to finish with a thin layer of foam.

Tip Most of a latte is milk, so the milk matters. Whole milk foams the best. Oat milk made for baristas is the best non-dairy swap.

Caramel latte

Hot Fancy⏱ 5 minutesMakes 1 drink
In Crema Rush · Level 3Espresso machine + Caramel + Milk steamer

You need

  • 1 double shot of espresso
  • 1 to 2 tbsp caramel sauce, plus a little for the top
  • 6 to 8 oz whole milk

Steps

  1. Squeeze the caramel into the bottom of the cup.
  2. Pull the espresso on top and stir until the caramel melts in.
  3. Steam the milk the same way as a latte.
  4. Pour in the milk, then add a crosshatch of caramel across the foam.

Tip Caramel sauce mixes in better than hard caramel candy. A pinch of flaky salt on top makes it a salted caramel latte.

Cinnamon latte

Hot⏱ 5 minutesMakes 1 drink
In Crema Rush · Level 4Espresso machine + Cinnamon + Milk steamer

You need

  • 1 double shot of espresso
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon, plus a pinch for dusting
  • 2 tsp sugar or maple syrup
  • 6 to 8 oz whole milk

Steps

  1. Put the cinnamon and sugar in the cup.
  2. Pull the espresso over them and stir well so the cinnamon doesn't clump.
  3. Steam the milk and pour it in.
  4. Dust a pinch of cinnamon over the foam.

Tip If you keep cinnamon in the milk jug while steaming, it blends in more smoothly. A cinnamon stick in the jug works too.

Mocha

Hot Fancy⏱ 5 minutesMakes 1 drink
In Crema Rush · Level 6Espresso machine + Chocolate + Milk steamer

You need

  • 1 double shot of espresso
  • 2 tbsp chocolate sauce (or 1 tbsp cocoa powder plus 1 tbsp sugar)
  • 6 oz whole milk

Steps

  1. Put the chocolate in the cup.
  2. Pull the espresso over it and stir into a smooth paste. If you're using cocoa powder, this is where it dissolves.
  3. Steam the milk and pour it in.
  4. Finish with a drizzle of chocolate.

Tip Dark chocolate sauce tastes more grown-up. Milk chocolate makes it sweeter, more like hot cocoa.

Mocha supreme

Hot Fancy⏱ 6 minutesMakes 1 drink
In Crema Rush · Level 7Espresso machine + Chocolate + Milk steamer + Whipped cream

You need

  • Everything in the Mocha
  • Whipped cream
  • Chocolate shavings or extra sauce

Steps

  1. Make a mocha, leaving about an inch of room at the top.
  2. Pipe or spoon a swirl of whipped cream on top.
  3. Drizzle chocolate over it and shave a little chocolate on top.

Tip Use a cup that holds 2 oz more than the drink, or the cream will slide down the side.

Drip coffee

Hot⏱ 5 minutesMakes 1 big mug (10 oz)
In Crema Rush · Level 2Drip brewer

You need

  • 20 g medium-ground coffee (about 3 tbsp)
  • 320 g water (about 11 oz) just off the boil, 195 to 205°F

Steps

  1. Rinse a paper filter with hot water and throw that water out.
  2. Add the grounds and pour just enough water to wet them. Wait 30 seconds while it bubbles (the bloom).
  3. Pour the rest slowly in circles over about 2 minutes.
  4. Let it drain. The whole brew should take 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. With a drip machine, use the same ratio: about 1 g of coffee per 16 g of water.

Tip The 1:16 ratio is the one number to remember. Scale it up for a full pot: 60 g of coffee for a liter of water.

🥐

Café au lait

Hot⏱ 5 minutesMakes 1 drink (8 oz)
In Crema Rush · Level 2Drip brewer + Milk steamer

You need

  • 4 oz strong drip or French press coffee
  • 4 oz whole milk

Steps

  1. Brew the coffee a little stronger than usual (about 1:14).
  2. Heat the milk on the stove or with a steamer until it steams, without boiling.
  3. Pour the coffee and milk into the cup at the same time, half and half.

Tip This is the French version of a latte: brewed coffee instead of espresso, and no foam to speak of. It's a good way to stretch a pot.

Cold brew

Cold⏱ 12 to 18 hours (mostly waiting)Makes About 6 glasses
In Crema Rush · Level 4Cold brew tower

You need

  • 100 g coarsely ground coffee (about 1 cup)
  • 800 ml cold filtered water (about 3 1/3 cups)
  • Ice

Steps

  1. Stir the grounds into the cold water in a jar or pitcher until they're all wet.
  2. Cover and leave in the fridge for 12 to 18 hours.
  3. Strain through a paper filter or a fine sieve lined with a cloth.
  4. This makes a concentrate. Mix it about 1 to 1 with water over ice.
  5. It keeps for about a week in the fridge.

Tip Grind it coarse, like breadcrumbs. Fine grounds make cold brew muddy and hard to strain.

Iced latte

Cold⏱ 3 minutesMakes 1 drink
In Crema Rush · Level 4Cold brew tower + Milk steamer

You need

  • 1 double shot of espresso, or 3 oz cold brew concentrate
  • 6 oz cold milk
  • Ice

Steps

  1. Fill a tall glass with ice.
  2. Pour in the cold milk.
  3. Pour the espresso or cold brew over the top so it layers, and stir before drinking.

Tip Crema Rush makes it with cold brew, which is the easy way at home. With hot espresso, pour it over the ice last so it chills fast.

Caramel frost

Cold Fancy⏱ 5 minutesMakes 1 drink
In Crema Rush · Level 7Cold brew tower + Caramel + Whipped cream

You need

  • 3 oz cold brew concentrate
  • 3 oz cold milk (optional)
  • 2 tbsp caramel sauce, plus some for the top
  • 1 cup ice
  • Whipped cream

Steps

  1. Blend the cold brew, milk, caramel and ice until smooth and thick. To make it on ice instead, shake it in a jar.
  2. Pour it into a tall glass.
  3. Top with whipped cream and a caramel drizzle.

Tip Freeze some cold brew in an ice tray. Cold brew cubes keep it from tasting watery as it melts.
