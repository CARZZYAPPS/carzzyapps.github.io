Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains what information Carzzy Apps ("we," "us") collects when you use carzzyapps.com, including our games, tools, accounts and leaderboard, and how we use it.

Carzzy Apps is made by Avis and DeBus. If you have questions about this policy, email us at carzzyapps@gmail.com.

By using the site, you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

Information we collect

Information you give us when you create an account

  • Email address
  • Username or display name
  • Password (stored in encrypted, hashed form; we can't see it)

Information from Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, Google shares basic profile information with us: your name, email address and profile picture. We don't get your Google password, and we don't access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or other Google data.

Information from using the site

  • Game scores, progress and leaderboard rankings
  • Settings and preferences you save in our tools

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site. It collects information such as pages visited, time spent, device and browser type, approximate location (city or country), and how you arrived at the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this.

We don't set any cookies of our own. To keep you signed in, Firebase saves a sign-in token in your browser's local storage.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account and sign you in
  • Save your game progress, scores and tool settings
  • Show rankings on the leaderboard
  • Understand how the site is used so we can fix problems and improve it
  • Respond to you when you contact us
  • Keep the site secure and prevent abuse or cheating

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

If you are in the European Union or the UK, we rely on these legal bases: providing the service you signed up for (your account, scores and leaderboard), our legitimate interest in improving and securing the site, and your consent for analytics cookies where the law requires it.

The leaderboard and public information

The leaderboard is public. Anyone visiting the site can see your display name and your scores or rankings.

We never show your email address on the leaderboard. Please choose a display name that doesn't include your full real name or other personal details. You can change your display name at any time in your account settings.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site:

  • Google Analytics — site usage statistics. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.
  • Google Sign-In — to sign you in if you choose that option.
  • Google Firebase — to store your account and game data. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, when you visit.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the site.

Cookies and how to opt out

The only cookies on our site come from Google Analytics. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, and the site will still work normally.

To stop Google Analytics from collecting your data, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How long we keep information and how we protect it

We keep your account information and scores for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal information right away, except where we must keep it by law. Google Analytics keeps detailed usage data for up to 14 months.

We use reasonable safeguards, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and hashed passwords, to protect your information. No website is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and choices

You can:

  • See and update your account information in your account settings
  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Delete your account and the data tied to it at any time using the delete button in your account settings
  • Opt out of analytics cookies (see Cookies and how to opt out)

Depending on where you live (for example, California, other US states, the EU or the UK), you may have additional rights, including the right to correct your data or object to how we use it. To make any request, email carzzyapps@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days, and we won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Children's privacy

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it and its information. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, please email carzzyapps@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. If we make significant changes, we'll let account holders know by email or a notice on the site.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: carzzyapps@gmail.com

You can also reach us through our Contact Us page.
