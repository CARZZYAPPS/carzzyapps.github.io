How to Play

Your mission is to crack the secret 4-color code! Tap any circle on your active row, then select a color from the panel.

Deciphering the Clues

After each guess, tiny clue pegs will reveal how close you are to cracking the code:

⚫   Black: Correct color in the correct spot.
⚪   White: Correct color, but in the wrong spot.
